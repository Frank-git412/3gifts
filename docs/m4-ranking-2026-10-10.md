# M4 first implementation block — 10 October 2026

M4 is in progress. M3 remains completed on the full catalog. This release implements the M2 weighted comparator and its diagnostic contract in the public research path; it does not claim complete note interpretation, quota-account verification, performance percentiles or M5 validation.

The public planner builds one common active-weight denominator per request: category 45, optional note 20, effect 15, occasion 12, relationship 8. Admitted lexical matches map to specific (1) or generic (0.5) category tiers. Occasion evidence for wedding, new baby and anniversary precedes category; personal mode disables the occasion. The comparator then uses category tier, weighted score, number of fully supported active signals, unrepresented subcategory and stable ASIN. Existing age-documented-first, interest coverage and duplicate filters remain. Scores are editorial, not probabilities.

Only M3 listing evidence supplies contextual values. Supported contributes 1, documented partial evidence 0.5, absent or contradictory 0. Missing product evidence never shrinks the denominator. Legacy `preferenceScore`, query frequency and ratings are not used by this public comparator. `chooseCandidate` remains available to the separate legacy/laboratory contract; the public M4 path explicitly disables reviews even when a synthetic object supplies them.

## Note boundary still open

Mandatory signals do not also earn the 20-point note bonus. The current browser note parser classifies many ordinary positive signals as `requiredSignals`; those keep their current hard-filter behavior and the note weight is inactive. The optional-note channel supports a bounded set of structured signals with affirmative listing evidence but is not a complete new note parser. Separating preferences, requests and obligations end to end is the next M4 task. Do not describe this block as completing all five input channels.

## Traces

Responses identify the ranking revision and active policy. Every admitted candidate receives five signal entries, evidence, contribution, numerator/denominator, category/event tier, and final decision. Each queried family has a query ID; final matches retain those IDs. The trace covers unique normalized candidates; raw pre-normalization exclusions still have aggregate counts. Raw visitor notes, names and exact deadlines are not added to logs or searches.

## Observed C25 defect

At 2026-10-10 07:36:50 UTC the public v124 C25 request returned three books, not the one reported on 8 October: 12 catalog calls, 0 cache hits, 72 received rows, 39 unique normalized candidates, 5 admitted, 30.22 seconds client time. Two selected age-unverified books were educational (Earth history and body boundaries) without established narrative evidence. This is not evidence that the earlier single-result cause was duplication, nor that the change in supply was caused by M4.

The stories-only branch now requires positive narrative evidence from title/features. Generic children's-book classification and the word “history” alone cannot fill the category. Explicit narrative treatment can still qualify an educational subject.

An intermediate v125 replay at 07:41:09 UTC reused all 12 cached searches and still returned three products. It removed the initial two false positives but exposed an encyclopedia whose feature text called learning an “adventure”, and a fill-in activity diary labeled 7–11 in the previously unrecognized form “dai 7 agli 11 anni”. Revision `20261010-m4-ranking2` excludes explicit encyclopedias/workbooks/activity diaries from the stories-only branch and recognizes `agli` in lower/upper age bounds. Narrative diaries such as a novel called “Diario di una schiappa” remain eligible when described as a novel. Regression fixtures preserve all four observed false-positive patterns. Live selection after this correction is recorded in the main progress report.

## Verification

`node scripts/validate-m4-ranking.mjs` records the current suite and command results in `docs/m4-ranking-validation.json`. Two UI revision assertions were updated from the literal M3 revision to the new M4 revision; behavioral assertions remain. An initial broad test glob also invoked old checkpoint generators that assert 22 still-pending expressions and 38 contextual families. Those historical expectations are obsolete after M3 closure (0 and 48); their source and historical reports are preserved, not rewritten to make a green result.

IT/EN active questionnaire explanations, Terms and Affiliations state that reviews are not used. Selected-product reasons describe category and price rather than commercial reliability; the public comparator rejects ratings as an ordering signal. This scoped audit does not review all editorial guides or certify product quality. No real browser or mobile visual QA was run for this backend-only change.

Remaining M4: end-to-end positive note contract; fuller normalization trace; combinatorial coverage edge cases; actual account quota and all consumers; representative timing measurements and observation. M5: frozen common-product comparison and editorial judgement of 120 scenarios.
