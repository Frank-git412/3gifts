# M5 corrections for G12 and G13

The frozen 120-scenario replay exposed eight note counterexamples and 14 wrong-type positions across five real-pool scenarios. S06 also selected two sizes of a massage cream under different families.

The note parser now composes recognized product nouns with properties for friendship novels, warm and dimmable reading lights, headsets for video calls, remote photo frames, fast-absorbing hand cream and two individual face masks. Positive preferences remain preferences. Negations, conditional claims, missing listing evidence and accessories cannot establish a requirement. Existing canonical M3 rules retain their stronger contracts.

Physical-book and Italian-language requirements are checked together. Bibliographic normalization accepts the Italian API labels for published language and author; an original-language field is not substituted for the published language. Already-read works become opaque normalized-title keys in the request, are checked across recognized editions and declared original titles, and survive refinement. This is a bounded title matcher, not a bibliographic authority or a universal translation resolver.

Headsets and remote frames require declared device compatibility before searching. The existing recovery panel asks for the missing connection, operating system or network details in Italian or English. Its layout is unchanged.

Massage, body and hand creams use a product-title function independent of their search family. Instructions about a masseur's hands do not classify a hand cream. Equivalent massage creams occupy one gift slot, including different sizes or ASINs.

Validation is reproducible with:

    node scripts/build-worker.mjs
    node scripts/validate-m5-fixes.mjs
    node tests/localization.cjs

The automated checks use simulated Amazon responses, including the actual assembled Worker and questionnaire code. They do not certify current Amazon availability, editorial quality or browser/mobile rendering. The 120 original requests and the original observation pool remain unchanged for the separate comparison. M5 editorial acceptance and the account-specific TPS/TPD confirmation remain open.
